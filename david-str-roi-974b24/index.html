Which looks best

  • Run it yourself: 1518 ($10,813/mo, 5.2%) and 2608 ($10,693/mo, 5.2%) are essentially tied.

  • With a 25% down loan, none of them cash flow at these prices and an 8.25% rate.

Things to keep in mind

  • 1518 may include 1520. The seller's expense sheet may cover 1518 and 1520 together, so its income may be for two units.
  • 1518 is understated on taxes. We count the seller's $33,920 of GET and TAT as a cost. 2608 and 2544 treat guest taxes as passed through to guests. On the same basis 1518 would be about $13,640 a month run yourself.
  • 2608's 2026 income is running 24% below 2025. The first half of 2026 was down from 2025, and July to October 2026 is not in yet.
  • 2544 can likely leave the hotel program. The Ilikai is resort zoned and allows owners to rent their own unit or use a manager. We have not yet confirmed the exit terms in 2544's current hotel agreement.
  • The loan is an assumption. 25% down, 8.25% for 30 years is our estimate, not a lender quote. Condotel lenders can ask for more down.
  • Estimates only. Electric, insurance, cable, cleaning and reserves are our estimates where marked; property tax uses the Hotel and Resort rate. Talk with your CPA about taxes. These are not promises of income or return.
