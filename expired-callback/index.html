Expired Callback Script

They called YOU back. Ask one question, affirm it, move on. Never quote a number on the call.
1. Opener
"Hi, this is Christopher with Talk Realty. Thanks for calling me back! I saw your home on [street] came off the market, and I was curious: are you still planning to move?"
If they ask "why did you reach out?": "Your home came off the market, and I wanted to see if you're still planning to move. Are you?"
2. Motivation (where, why, when)
"If it had sold, where were you planning to move?"
Affirm: "Oh nice, that's a great area."
"What's got you wanting to make that move?"
Affirm: "That makes total sense."
"Is that still the plan, or has anything changed?"
"When would you ideally like to be there?"
Affirm: "Got it, so there's a real timeline."
"How important is it to make that happen?"
3. Why it didn't sell
"What do you think kept it from selling?"
Let them vent. Don't defend or blame the last agent.
"Were there showings? What feedback did you get?"
"That's really frustrating, especially after all those showings and keeping the house ready."
"What would you do differently this time?"
4. Gap and consequence
"So if it had sold, you'd already be in [place] right now?"
"What happens if it sits for another 6 months?"
Ask, then pause. Let them fill the silence.
5. Value offer
"Here's what I'd like to do. I'll put together a free net sheet showing what you'd actually walk away with at today's prices, plus what I'd do differently on pricing, photos and marketing. Would it hurt to take a look?"
6. Close
"It takes about 20 minutes. Would later today or tomorrow work better?"
Then lock a specific time, read back their email, confirm the mobile number for a reminder.
7. Objections
"We're taking a break."
"Makes sense. When you do come back, what would need to be different? And if nothing changes in the next 6 months, how does that affect your move?"
"We're staying with our old agent."
"Great. Are you signed with them again, or just talking? If you're signed, I'll step back. If not, what would you want them to do differently?"
"The price was the problem." / "Your price will be too low."
"I hear you, and I won't guess on the phone. What do you need to walk away with? The net sheet shows exactly that, so you can decide with real numbers."
"Just send me some info."
"Happy to. I'll text the net sheet over and we can go through it together in 10 minutes. Does [time] work?"
"I'm not interested."
"Totally fair. Is that not interested in selling at all, or just not interested in going through what happened last time?"
"The last agent let us down."
"That sounds frustrating. What should they have done differently? If someone did that, would you be open to trying again?"
"I need to talk to my spouse."
"Of course. Would it make sense for both of you to be on the 20 minutes so nobody has to repeat it? What works for both of you?"
"We'll sell it ourselves."
"Could be a smart move. What makes you feel that would work better this time? Would it hurt to see your net both ways before you decide?"
"We'll just wait / maybe rent it."
"That can make sense. Would it help to see what it could rent for alongside the net sheet, so you have both numbers?"
Never
Quote a price or rent on the call · put down the last agent · lead with renting · contact anyone who asked to stop (tag DNC).
