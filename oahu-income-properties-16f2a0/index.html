Good to know

How the returns work
  • Cash return (cap rate) = yearly net operating income ÷ price. With a loan: (NOI − mortgage) ÷ cash you put in (down payment + about 2% closing). DSCR = NOI ÷ mortgage; lenders usually want 1.20 to 1.25. Listings that lose money in your scenario are hidden.
  • Duplex to fourplex default 25% down, 7.75%, 30 years: Freddie Mac 30 year average 7.18% (Nov 5, 2026) plus an investment premium.
  • 5+ units, mixed use and commercial default 30% down, 7.75%, 25 year amortization: 10 year Treasury 4.98% (Nov 10, 2026) plus a typical 2.5 to 3 point commercial spread. Commercial loans often balloon in 5 to 10 years.
Where the income comes from
  • Listing-reported: the NOI, cap rate or rents stated in the MLS listing, with its basis. When there are several, the lower one is used. Sellers' figures often leave out vacancy, management or reserves, so ask for the rent roll and 24 months of statements.
  • Estimate: HUD 2026 Fair Market Rents for Urban Honolulu by bedroom ($1,877 studio, $2,016 one bd, $2,642 two bd, $3,674 three bd), lower for average or fair condition and pre 1960 buildings.
  • Estimate costs: 5% vacancy, GET 4.712%, property tax by zoning, insurance $1,500 a unit, repairs 8%, reserves 5%, utilities $120 a unit a month, accounting, 8% manager.
Condition and update estimates
  • Condition is our estimate from the MLS condition field, the listing remarks and year built: Turnkey, Light refresh, Moderate update or Major renovation.
  • Update cost per square foot (Honolulu): Light refresh $45 to $70 (RenoKalk Honolulu cosmetic scope, RSMeans Honolulu cost index 1.22); Moderate update $125 to $195 (Warrior Construction Hawaii estimator, condo renovation, Oct 2026 pricing); Major renovation $235 to $370 (Warrior Construction, full home remodel, Oct 2026 pricing). Permits and contingency extra.
  • Value lift uses the share of cost that comes back at resale in the 2025 Cost vs. Value report (Remodeling magazine / Zonda): small updates about 90% to 120%, moderate 50% to 90%, major 35% to 57%.
  • Photos open the property on Zillow when we found it there; buttons go to Christopher's site.
  • Rent lift after updates comes from our own condition adjustment: Light refresh 0% to 3%, Moderate 5% to 11%, Major 11% to 25%. Living area from the MLS, or 750 sf per unit when missing.
  • Upkeep reserve is taken out of every return shown: $300 (Turnkey), $400 (Light), $600 (Moderate), $900 (Major) per unit a year, above the Fannie Mae multifamily minimum of $250. Commercial: $0.25 per building sq ft a year.

Tips for new investors
  • Compare cap rates first, then test the loan view. Many buildings only cash flow with a bigger down payment today.
  • Price per unit and rent per unit tell you more than total price.
  • Duplex to fourplex: living in one unit can open owner occupant loans. Ask a lender.
  • Check leases: month to month tenants, below market rents and who pays utilities change the real return.
  • Before an offer: rent roll, 24 months of statements, permits, zoning, inspection. Talk with a CPA and an attorney.
