Good to know

How the returns work
  • Cash return = yearly net income ÷ price. With a loan: (net income − mortgage) ÷ cash you put in (down payment + about 2% closing). Listings that lose money in your scenario are hidden.
  • Default loan 25% down, 7.75%, 30 years: Freddie Mac's 30 year average 7.18% (Nov 5, 2026) plus a typical investment loan premium.
  • Est. total return adds the area's 10 year yearly appreciation (with a loan, one year of appreciation and principal paydown on your cash).
Where the income comes from
  • Seller-reported: real income the seller gave, with its period. Ilikai #1518 uses the owner's Airbnb and VRBO payouts (2025). Ala Moana Hotel units use the hotel's owner statements, the lower of the last full year and the 2026 pace.
  • Estimate: BNBCalc trailing 12 month average revenue for the area and bedroom count (Nov 3, 2026).
  • Costs taken out: 20% manager (or self managed), cleaning, HOA, property tax at $13.90 per $1,000, insurance, utilities, supplies, registration, accounting, and a 5% upkeep reserve. Ala Moana Hotel: owner net minus HOA, tax, insurance and a 5% reserve.
Condition and update estimates
  • Condition is our estimate from the MLS condition field, the listing remarks and year built: Turnkey, Light refresh, Moderate update or Major renovation.
  • Update cost per square foot (Honolulu): Light refresh $45 to $70 (RenoKalk Honolulu cosmetic scope, RSMeans Honolulu cost index 1.22); Moderate update $125 to $195 (Warrior Construction Hawaii estimator, condo renovation, Oct 2026 pricing); Major renovation $235 to $370 (Warrior Construction, full home remodel, Oct 2026 pricing). Permits and contingency extra.
  • Value lift uses the share of cost that comes back at resale in the 2025 Cost vs. Value report (Remodeling magazine / Zonda): small updates about 90% to 120%, moderate 50% to 90%, major 35% to 57%.
  • Photos open the property on Zillow when we found it there; buttons go to Christopher's site.
  • Rental income lift after updates is our assumption: Light refresh 0% to 5%, Moderate 5% to 10%, Major 10% to 20%.
  • Upkeep reserve (5% of rental income) is already in every return shown so the numbers hold up over time.
Area appreciation
  • Ala Moana: 3.1% a year (5 yr), 3.5% a year (10 yr). Oahu wide condo median, HBR (2015 $360K, 2020 $435K, 2025 $507.3K). The Ala Moana to Kakaako median is skewed by new luxury towers, so the island figure is used.
  • Ko Olina: 4.0% a year (5 yr), 4.7% a year (10 yr). Ewa Plain condo median, HBR (2015 $409K, 2020 $530K, 2025 $645K).
  • Makaha: 4.9% a year (5 yr), 5.8% a year (10 yr). Makaha to Nanakuli single family median, HBR (2015 $380K, 2020 $524K, 2025 $665K).
  • Turtle Bay (North Shore): 8.0% a year (5 yr), 6.4% a year (10 yr). North Shore region condo median, HBR (2015 $387.5K, 2020 $487.9K, 2025 $717.5K; small sample, about 36 to 72 sales a year).
  • Waikiki: 2.4% a year (5 yr), 2.3% a year (10 yr). Waikiki condo median, Honolulu Board of REALTORS (2015 $349K, 2020 $390K, 2025 $440K).
  • Honolulu Board of REALTORS® full year medians 2015, 2020, 2025. Past appreciation does not predict future prices.
Which listings are here
  • Active fee simple listings up to $3M where nightly rental is allowed: Waikiki resort zoned and NUC units, Ala Moana Hotel through its rental program, Turtle Bay, Ko Olina Beach Villas, Makaha Mauna Olu cottages. Leasehold excluded. Data Nov 11, 2026.
Tips for new investors
  • Confirm nightly use for the exact unit: zoning or a current NUC, AOAO rules, any hotel program terms.
  • Ask for 24 months of statements.
  • Talk with a CPA about taxes and an attorney about legal questions.
